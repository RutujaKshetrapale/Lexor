\# LEXOR



\## AI-Powered Smart Mobility Platform



> \*\*Intelligent Mobility. Elevated.\*\*



LEXOR is a next-generation smart mobility platform designed to provide intelligent, scalable, and data-driven transportation services.



The platform connects customers, drivers, vehicles, and administrators through a modern distributed architecture while integrating real-time communication, AI/ML, automation, analytics, IoT, Digital Twin technology, and blockchain.



\---



\## 🚀 Vision



LEXOR aims to transform traditional ride-hailing into an intelligent mobility ecosystem capable of:



\- Real-time ride booking

\- Intelligent driver matching

\- Real-time vehicle tracking

\- Dynamic pricing

\- AI-powered predictions

\- Automated workflows

\- Vehicle Digital Twins

\- IoT telemetry simulation

\- Blockchain-based verification

\- Business intelligence and analytics



\---



\## 👥 Platform Roles



\### Customer



\- Register and authenticate

\- Search and book rides

\- Track drivers in real time

\- View fare estimates

\- Make payments

\- Rate and review rides

\- Submit feedback

\- Access ride history



\### Driver



\- Register and complete profile

\- Manage vehicle information

\- Go online/offline

\- Receive ride requests

\- Accept or reject rides

\- Navigate to pickup

\- Start and complete trips

\- View earnings

\- Track vehicle health



\### Admin



\- Manage customers and drivers

\- Monitor rides

\- Monitor vehicles

\- View platform analytics

\- Monitor payments

\- Review feedback

\- Monitor AI insights

\- Monitor Digital Twin simulations

\- Manage operational workflows
